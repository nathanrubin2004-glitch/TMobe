01 — The Man

Who Is Coach Tyrell?

29years in the game

Twenty-nine years in the game. Over a decade on the sidelines. Tyrell Mosley grew up with a ball in his hands on Boston blacktops — and he hasn’t put it down since.

At MassBay Community College, he led the nation in 3-point percentage at 60%, finishing his career with over 1,000 points scored and a degree in Business with a concentration in Sports Management.

From there, he stepped into coaching as an assistant at his alma mater, Massachusetts College of Liberal Arts, before spending seven years building players with the Bay State Jaguars club program. Five years ago, he founded NGUNQ — Never Give Up, Never Quit — and now trains athletes from ages 5 through 25 across the city that raised him.

For Tyrell, this isn’t a job. It’s a calling.

“His passion and love for the kids is beyond measure.”
